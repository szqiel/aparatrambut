## Brand & Style

This design system drives a bespoke, single-chair grooming atelier experience. Tailored for discerning clients seeking an intentional, ritualistic haircutting service, the interface shifts away from standard transactional appointment booking toward high-fashion editorial art direction. 

The aesthetic is grounded in **Neo-Editorial Minimalism** infused with technical digital-studio rigor:
- **Atmosphere:** Intimate, quiet, and hyper-curated. The design draws directly from independent print publications, architectural portfolios, and high-end studio index layouts.
- **Visual Tension:** Created through extreme typographic scale juxtapositions—pairing monumental display headers with pinpoint monospace structural timestamps and metadata tags.
- **Geometry & Framing:** Relying on razor-sharp 1px structural hairline rules, strict asymmetric grids, sparse navigation, and tactile circular booking anchors rather than standard card UI.
- **Tone:** Uncompromising, modern, and artisanal. Every interaction feels deliberate, calm, and exclusive.

## Colors

The palette operates in a dedicated, high-contrast dark environment inspired by dimly lit studio interiors, raw concrete, and cinematic spotlighting.

- **Foundational Background (`#202020`):** A deep, warm charcoal matte that eliminates glare while providing a canvas richer than pitch black.
- **Surface Layer (`#282828`):** Barely-stepped tonal value applied selectively to interactive zones and drawer containers.
- **Primary Accent (`#F05A7E`):** An electric editorial pink used with extreme restraint—reserved for focal selection states, live slot status indicators, and micro indicator nodes.
- **Secondary / Soft Accent (`#442831`):** A muted, deep wine-pink tone used for contextual badge backgrounds, active list row highlights, and delicate focus frames.
- **Structural Lines (`#3A3638`):** A calibrated hairline tone establishing the spatial architecture without breaking visual quietness.
- **Primary Text (`#F3F0F1`):** Bone white, avoiding harsh absolute white to sustain an editorial print feel.
- **Muted Text (`#8E888B`):** Cool slate gray for secondary captions, session metadata, and auxiliary technical specifications.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy is engineered around dual-type dynamics: sculptural geometric sans-serif headers balanced by hyper-precise, technical monospace notations.

### Display & Headlines
Set in **Plus Jakarta Sans** with negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.035em`) and tightly packed leading. Headlines must feel architectural and commanding, reminiscent of large-format mastheads. Use Sentence Case or all-lowercase for stylistic variation in subheadings; reserve ALL CAPS strictly for labels.

### Structural Monospace & Metadata
Set in **JetBrains Mono**. Used universally for operational information: appointment times (`14:00 - 15:15`), session durations (`[ 45 MIN ]`), currency units (`IDR 350K`), and index numbering (`01 // HAIRCUT`).

### Section Marker Pattern
Section titles must be preceded by an uppercase micro-label set in `meta-label`, accompanied by an inline 6px circular dot (`accent-pink`). Example: `● INDEX / SERVICES`.

## Layout & Spacing

Layout execution relies on an **Asymmetrical Structural Grid** bounded by hairlines rather than boxed modules.

### Grid Framework
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column grid with `3rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Columns regularly combine into asymmetrical arrangements (e.g., 4 columns for metadata/sticky navigators, 8 columns for live schedule and narrative imagery).
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with `2rem` margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1.25rem` margins. Rows stack vertically, demarcated by full-bleed top-and-bottom hairline dividers (`#3A3638`).

### Negative Space Rhythm
Generous macro-spacing (`space-3xl` to `space-editorial`) between distinct editorial narrative blocks creates deliberate pauses, forcing focus onto single-chair exclusivity. Micro-spacing (`space-xs` to `space-md`) within monospace tags and details maintains technical density.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system purposefully excludes blurred drop shadows, multi-tier elevation halos, and generic skeuomorphic float effects. Depth is expressed strictly through **Architectural Layering**:

1. **Hairline Delimitation:** Depth is planar. Sections are separated by crisp `1px solid #3A3638` rules running either horizontally edge-to-edge or vertically between grid zones.
2. **Surface Shifts:** Modals, side drawers, and sheet overlays make use of subtle background stepped tone (`#282828`) over the base canvas (`#202020`), encased by a continuous 1px outline (`#3A3638`).
3. **Orbital Accents:** Circular decorative wireframes (e.g., 200px rings rendered in `1px solid #3A3638`) sit discreetly in the background, reinforcing technical precision.
4. **Overlay Dimming:** Backdrops for active booking overlays use `rgba(32, 32, 32, 0.85)` accompanied by a sharp `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)` to sustain focus on the active task while preserving ambient context.

## Shapes

The primary geometry is **Sharp (0px radius)**, emphasizing architectural structure and brutalist precision. 

- **Containers, Panels & Inputs:** Straight 90-degree corners with 1px hairline edges. No rounded corners on inputs, dropdown menus, or content blocks.
- **The Circular Exception:** High-intent action triggers (e.g., "BOOK CHAIR", "SELECT DATE") and status indicators break the orthogonal grid using pure **circles (1:1 aspect ratio, `border-radius: 50%`)**. This dual dialogue between rigid straight axes and perfect circles forms the signature visual language of the studio.

## Components

### Buttons & Circular CTAs
- **Primary Studio CTA:** Circular button (120px to 160px diameter desktop; 96px mobile) with an outer `1px solid #3A3638` ring. Filled with `#F3F0F1` or `#F05A7E`, displaying centered uppercase text in `meta-label`. On hover, the ring scales outward slightly with smooth linear damping.
- **Secondary Action:** Rectangular button, 0px border radius, background transparent, border `1px solid #3A3638`, text color `#F3F0F1`. Hover switches background to `#442831` and border to `#F05A7E`.
- **Text Link:** Underlined with a thin 1px offset line, accompanied by a trailing monospace glyph (`->`).

### Editorial Service List
- Instead of cards, services (Haircut, Beard Sculpt, Ritual Treatment) are rendered as full-width index rows bounded by horizontal hairlines (`#3A3638`).
- Row contents: index number (`01`), service title in `headline-sm`, duration in `meta-code`, and price in `meta-time`.
- Hover state: Entire row subtly shifts background to `#282828` with an accent dot (`#F05A7E`) appearing beside the service title.

### Time Slot Selectors (Chips)
- Sharp rectangular chips (`border: 1px solid #3A3638`, background `#202020`, font `meta-time`).
- Disabled slots feature muted text (`#8E888B`) with a diagonal strikethrough.
- Selected state fills with `#F05A7E`, setting typography to `#202020` in bold monospace.

### Form Inputs & Text Fields
- Minimalist underline or boxed hairline inputs (`1px solid #3A3638`), zero radius.
- Background: `#282828` or completely transparent.
- Placeholder text in `#8E888B`. Active focus transitions border to `#F05A7E`. Labels rendered in `meta-label` above the field in uppercase.

### Status Indicators & Toggles
- Single-chair status displays a live pulse dot: a 6px circle `#F05A7E` with an optional outer ping ring indicating studio availability.
- Radio buttons and checkboxes avoid rounded bubbles: checkboxes use a square 14px box with a solid `#F05A7E` square fill when checked; radio elements use a clean circular border housing an inner accent dot.
## Final Product Revision

The exported screens are the source for development, not a luxury-studio concept board. Keep the editorial structure but use plain, human Indonesian copy and only information that helps a customer book or helps the kapster run today's agenda.

- All three services are exactly Rp50.000 and 60 menit.
- Do not invent addresses, coordinates, staff names, statistics, membership claims, or service prices.
- Use `Booking sekarang`, `Pilih layanan`, `Pilih waktu`, `Kunci booking`, `Ubah jadwal`, and `Batalkan booking` as direct action language.
- Replace atelier, ritual, accession, protocol, concierge, archive, and system-coded language with normal Indonesian.
- The main CTA is a soft rectangular button with a 12px radius. Do not use large circular CTA buttons or circular booking controls; those are reserved as a signature of the external OKC reference.
- Status dots may remain circular, but they are not buttons.
- The light theme must use the same layout, typography, spacing, pink accent, and component shapes as dark mode. Only surface and text colors change.
- The light theme must never inherit dark charcoal surfaces from the dark theme.
- Customer content is concise and personal: explain what the service is, when to arrive, how to pay, and what happens next.
- Admin content is operational: show time, customer, service, status, and the next action. Remove decorative metrics and unnecessary jargon.
- Use only text actions, simple dots, arrows, and structural rules. Do not load a generic icon library.
- Placeholder address and contact values must be visibly marked as placeholders until the owner supplies real values.
